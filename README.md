# ZeepalmLabs Health Components

A collection of Flutter projects, each in its own folder.

## Projects

- `safe_space` - Soft pastel onboarding screen with floating community cards and staggered entrance animations.
- `fitonist` - Dark fitness onboarding flow with ScreenUtil, GetX, Google Fonts, and Iconsax — pixel-perfect UI with image placeholders.
- `pillora` - Medication reminders with a painted, animated pill-city onboarding, liquid week strip and nav, and an AI helper with a glass-orb fragment shader and voice mode.
- `glowlens` - AI wrinkle detection with a live face mesh and laser scan, holographic shader cards, animated score rings and trend chart, and a skincare shop with liquid filters and fly-to-cart.
- `aira_agent` - Voice-first AI agent builder: a morphing ember background, a molten shader orb that blooms from the mic, live karaoke transcript, and a chat where the transcript becomes the first bubble and the orb becomes Aira's avatar.
- `care_circle` - Family care circle: avatars that fly from a floating welcome orbit into a living family orbit, member vitals and a shared timeline, gentle nudges, and a medication sheet with an animated dose ring.
- `nocturne` - Premium sleep sound mixer: nine living shader orbs, streams that pour into a glass vessel whose nebula mixes by volume, synthesized seamless ambience loops, and a calm sleep mode with a lights-out dim.
- `trailglow` - Night-running concept on a real Mapbox map: a Manhattan street grid rebuilt in code so mock routes land on actual streets, a glowing trail that grows as a simulated run progresses over 3D buildings, a pace chart and splits, and a lifetime heatmap.
- `ember_journal` - Ember Journal: shake for a reflective question, answer it, tag the mood, and watch the entry fly into your journal.
- `fit_journey` - FitJourney: a city-fitness app with a logo that builds itself, a pin-shaped portal into split-flap onboarding, odometer goal stats, and a live route that draws itself around the lake before a runner follows it.
- `move_quest` - MoveQuest: a fitness adventure with a hex-portal splash, activity discs thrown from the runner's hand, a living quest map with falling landmark badges and energy paths, and a quest card that expands into a parallax route detail.
- `pocket_chef` - PocketChef: a pixel-matched recipe app with a furry chef mascot, a splash where vegetables juggle out of the pan, a tilting Today's Pick card, a card-to-page morph into a detail page whose sheet settles like liquid, falling parmesan, count-up nutrition and a profile with a flipping gold coin.
- `momo` - Momo: a food diary with a code-drawn peach companion that tracks your finger and talks, a camera whose photo flies into the chat in 3D, a meal card that morphs from typing dots and rolls its calories like a slot machine, and a calendar sheet that carries the mascot up with a cascading month swap.
- `ascend` - Ascend: a habit-tracker onboarding with a level ring that fills while the level counts up, a habit card that flies in and springs into place, a hexagon streak badge that spins in and counts to 100 inside growing laurels, and a podium whose columns rise before the friends' avatars drop onto them.
- `orbit` - Orbit: a planet-growing fitness app matched to its mockup, with a real-time 3D planet shader you can spin and fling, an egg that hatches into your world, a live run screen with coins that fill a boost bar and a volcano chest, and a weekly orbit race where a rocket carries your challenge to a friend.
- `streak_rpg` - StreakRPG: a fitness RPG matched to its mockup, with a logo that slams in on a shockwave while the crown drops on, a burning portal into a hex badge that levels up from Lv. 1 to 5 and flings out power-up orbs, a hero who beams onto his cliff and does real push-ups that count into the quest bar, an odometer workout timer with a heart-rate pod and combo meter, quests that stamp, pour and fly their XP to your level, a game dock whose orb hops between sockets, and Coach Kai's spotlight tutorials with tap-to-try steps.
- `helora_duo` - Helora: a calm health dashboard that reflows from a phone column with tabs to an open iPhone Duo spread split around the real hinge, with a dark resting-heart-rate hero, scrubbable pill-bar trends, stacked sleep stages, an HRV dot grid, and medication names that only appear when the phone is open.
